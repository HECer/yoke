# Review the engineering period

Confirm the requested date range, repositories, and question. Use available commits, PRs, CI results, incidents, and accepted outcomes. State coverage gaps and timezone. Deduplicate merges and distinguish delivery dates from author dates.

Summarize shipped behavior, incomplete work, regressions, recoveries, and repeated friction. Prefer cycle time, failure/rework rate, and accepted outcomes when measured. Commit volume, lines changed, and model tokens alone do not establish productivity or quality; do not rank contributors or infer personal traits from them.

Investigate the few patterns supported by evidence. Link representative changes and distinguish direct observations from hypotheses. Recommend a small set of actions with an owner or decision point, expected benefit, and a way to check improvement.

Keep raw logs and large tables outside the narrative when a compact summary and pointers suffice. Do not call a model repeatedly to summarize the same history or create a dashboard without a stated need. Make repository changes only if the request includes them.
